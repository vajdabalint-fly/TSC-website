STAFF APPLICATION

INTERVIEW REQUIREMENTS

Before joining the staff team, every applicant must meet the requirements and successfully complete the interview.

01

APPLICANT REQUIREMENTS

2+ WEEKS Must have been in The Social Circle for at least two weeks.
ENGLISH Must be able to communicate in English well enough for full conversations.
1+ HOUR Must be available for at least one hour per day.
17+ Must be at least 17 years old and willing to complete age verification.
02

BASIC INTERVIEW RULES

24 HOURS The interview must be completed within 24 hours of starting it.
VERIFY Age verification must be completed after the interview and before being hired.
TRAINING All successful applicants must complete staff training after being hired.
ENGLISH English must be spoken or written throughout the entire interview.
RECORD No recent punishments. At least one week must have passed since a punishment.
03
INTERVIEW RESULTS

WHEN DO YOU FAIL?

01

Not completing the interview within 24 hours or leaving during a voice interview.

02

Getting more than three answers wrong.

03

Using disrespectful language or refusing to speak English during the interview.

04

Failing or refusing to complete age verification.
